Privacy

If you fill in our join form, we receive your name, email and any other details you choose to give us. We use them only to welcome you to the club and tell you about club runs. We don't sell or share them.

Form submissions are stored securely by our website host, Netlify. To have your details removed, message us on Facebook and we'll delete them.
