Your first run

Nervous? Everyone was. Here's exactly what happens.

All levels welcome

Track drills, social runs and trail runs every Wednesday and Sunday. Some of us race marathons and fell races, some of us are building up to our first 5k. You'll fit right in.

Before

Pick a run

Wednesday track is a great first choice — fixed place, easy to find, and you can drop out any lap. Check this week's runs.

On the night

Arrive early

Get there 5–10 minutes before. Look for the group, say it's your first time, and we'll introduce you.

After

Stay for one

Pub on Wednesdays, coffee on Sundays. That's where the friendships (and the race entries) happen.

FAQs

Questions we get asked

I'm a beginner — can I come?

Yes. Nob End Run Club is a fun, friendly social club for all abilities. We'd much rather you came along than waited until you feel 'ready'.

Will I get left behind?

We look out for each other. On road and trail runs we regroup along the way, and on the track nobody is ever far away. Tell us it's your first run and we'll make sure someone runs with you.

When and where do you run?

Every Wednesday evening (track at Leverhulme Park, meet 7:50pm for an 8pm start; or a 7:30pm road/trail run that finishes at a pub) and every Sunday (road, trail or canal run, then coffee). Start points change weekly and are posted on our Facebook page and the Our Runs page.

Do I need to book?

No. You can just turn up. Filling in the join form helps us know to look out for you.

What should I bring?

Running shoes, weather-appropriate kit, water if you like it, and on dark evenings something bright or reflective plus a head torch for trail runs. A few quid for a drink or coffee afterwards.

How far do you run?

It varies by session — track nights run for about an hour, and road, trail and canal runs are a sociable distance. If you're worried about distance, message us on Facebook and we'll tell you what's planned.

Where is Nob End?

Nob End Nature Reserve is in Little Lever, Bolton, Greater Manchester, where the Croal meets the Irwell next to the Manchester, Bolton & Bury Canal.

How do I keep up with club news?

Follow our Facebook page — that's where weekly start points, socials and race plans go up first.
