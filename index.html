Every week

Wednesdays & Sundays

Two club runs a week. Come to one, or come to all of them.

Wednesday · Track

Track night

7:50pm

Leverhulme Park athletics track, Bolton. Meet at 7:50pm, start at 8pm. About an hour of drills and reps on the 400m track, all at your own pace.

Wednesday · Road / Trail

Pub run

7:30pm

When we're not on the track we run the roads around Bolton, and the trails when the nights are lighter. The start point changes each week and we usually finish at a pub.

Sunday · Road, trail or canal

Sunday miles, coffee & cake

9:00am

A road, trail or canal towpath run around Nob End and Little Lever, then coffee and cake. We start at 9am unless we say otherwise, and the start point goes up on our socials.

Where we run

A running club for Bolton and beyond

We're based at Nob End Nature Reserve in Little Lever, between Bolton, Farnworth, Kearsley and Radcliffe. Track nights are at Leverhulme Park in Bolton, and our road, trail and canal routes take in Prestolee, the Irwell Valley and the Manchester, Bolton and Bury Canal.

It's easy to get to us from Bury, Salford and Manchester. Leverhulme Park is roughly half an hour by car from Manchester city centre, so if you're looking for a friendly running club in the North West that isn't all about pace, come and try a session.
