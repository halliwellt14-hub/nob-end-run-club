Every week

Wednesdays & Sundays

Two club runs a week, three flavours. Pick one, pick all of them.

Wednesday · Track

Track night

7:50pm

Leverhulme Park athletics track, Bolton. Meet 7:50pm, we're off at 8pm for about an hour. Structured, sociable and great for getting quicker — every pace has a place on a 400m loop.

Wednesday · Road / Trail

Pub run

7:30pm

On non-track weeks we head out on the roads around Bolton — and onto the trails in the lighter months. Start point changes each week and we usually finish at a pub for a well-earned drink.

Sunday · Road, trail or canal

Sunday miles, coffee & cake

Sunday

Road, trail or a canal-towpath run around Nob End, Little Lever and beyond — then coffee and cake afterwards. The perfect way to ease into the week. Time and start point posted on Facebook.

First-timer guide

Community

Without its people, there is no run club.

Nob End Run Club isn't a route or a time on a watch — it's the people who turn up. The ones who wait at the end of the rep, shout you up the last hill and save you a seat at the pub. Every new face makes the club stronger.

All abilities

First-timers, returners, marathoners and ultra runners all run together. Nobody is too slow.

All ages & people

Whoever you are, you're welcome. Come on your own — you won't be on your own for long.

Good people, stronger together

Running is better shared. The miles, the mud, the pint and the cake afterwards.

Run leader

Meet Jimmy Hark

🏅 Bolton Civic Medal recipient, 2025

Jimmy leads Nob End Run Club. A personal trainer and gym co-founder, he has built free, welcoming spaces where people of every age and ability can get active and make friends for life.

In December 2025 he was awarded the Bolton Civic Medal — the borough's highest civic honour — in recognition of what the club has done for people across Bolton.
