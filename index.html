Every week

Wednesdays & Sundays

Two club runs a week, three flavours. Pick one, pick all of them.

Wednesday · Track

Track night

7:50pm

Leverhulme Park athletics track, Bolton. Meet 7:50pm, we're off at 8pm for about an hour. Structured, sociable and great for getting quicker — every pace has a place on a 400m loop.

Wednesday · Road / Trail

Pub run

7:30pm

On non-track weeks we head out on the roads around Bolton — and onto the trails in the lighter months. Start point changes each week and we usually finish at a pub for a well-earned drink.

Sunday · Road, trail or canal

Sunday run & coffee

Sunday

Road, trail or a canal-towpath run around Nob End, Little Lever and beyond — then coffee afterwards. The perfect way to ease into the week. Time and start point posted on Facebook.
