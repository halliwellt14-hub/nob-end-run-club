What happens when

Wednesday · Track

Track night

7:50pm

Leverhulme Park athletics track, Bolton. Meet at 7:50pm, start at 8pm. About an hour of drills and reps on the 400m track, all at your own pace.

Wednesday · Road / Trail

Pub run

7:30pm

When we're not on the track we run the roads around Bolton, and the trails when the nights are lighter. The start point changes each week and we usually finish at a pub.

Sunday · Road, trail or canal

Sunday miles, coffee & cake

9:00am

A road, trail or canal towpath run around Nob End and Little Lever, then coffee and cake. We start at 9am unless we say otherwise, and the start point goes up on our socials.

Track nights at Leverhulme Park

Track sessions are the one fixed location in our week: Leverhulme Park athletics track, Bolton. Meet at 7:50pm, warm up together and we start at 8pm for roughly an hour. Everyone works at their own effort, and on a 400m track nobody is ever far away.

Wednesday pub runs

When we're not on the track, Wednesday is a road run around Bolton at about 7:30pm, and in the lighter months we often head onto the trails. The route changes each week and almost always finishes at a local pub.

Sunday runs, coffee and cake

Sundays start at 9am unless we say otherwise, and they're relaxed: a road, trail or canal-towpath run around Nob End, Little Lever, Prestolee and the Irwell Valley, followed by coffee and cake. A good way to build your weekly miles with company.

Where is Nob End?

Nob End is a nature reserve and Site of Special Scientific Interest in Little Lever, Bolton, between the River Croal, the River Irwell and the Manchester, Bolton & Bury Canal. It's the club's home patch, with trails, towpaths and wildflower meadows on the doorstep.
