What happens when

Wednesday · Track

Track night

7:50pm

Leverhulme Park athletics track, Bolton. Meet 7:50pm, we're off at 8pm for about an hour. Structured, sociable and great for getting quicker — every pace has a place on a 400m loop.

Wednesday · Road / Trail

Pub run

7:30pm

On non-track weeks we head out on the roads around Bolton — and onto the trails in the lighter months. Start point changes each week and we usually finish at a pub for a well-earned drink.

Sunday · Road, trail or canal

Sunday run & coffee

Sunday

Road, trail or a canal-towpath run around Nob End, Little Lever and beyond — then coffee afterwards. The perfect way to ease into the week. Time and start point posted on Facebook.

Track nights at Leverhulme Park

Track sessions are the one fixed location in our week: Leverhulme Park athletics track, Bolton. Meet at 7:50pm, warm up together and we start at 8pm for roughly an hour. Sessions are designed so everyone works at their own effort — you'll never be lapped and left behind, because on a track nobody is ever far away.

Wednesday pub runs

When we're not on the track, Wednesday is a road run around Bolton at about 7:30pm — and in the lighter months we often head onto the trails. The route changes each week and almost always finishes at a local pub. Running optional for the drink, drink optional for the run.

Sunday runs & coffee

Sundays are relaxed: a road, trail or canal-towpath run around Nob End, Little Lever, Prestolee and the Irwell Valley, followed by coffee. A brilliant way to build your weekly miles with company.

Where is Nob End?

Nob End is a nature reserve and Site of Special Scientific Interest in Little Lever, Bolton, between the River Croal, the River Irwell and the Manchester, Bolton & Bury Canal. It's the club's home patch — fantastic trails, towpaths and wildflower meadows on our doorstep.
